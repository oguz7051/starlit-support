Frequently asked questions

Does Starlit work offline?

Yes. Focus sessions and your progress are designed to work without an internet connection.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Starlit does not require an account. Your app data is stored locally on your device.

Is Starlit a subscription?

No. Starlit is offered as a one-time paid download, without a recurring subscription.

Can I use Starlit while my phone is locked?

Starlit supports focus timing in the background and can show a live countdown on supported iPhone lock screens and Dynamic Island devices.

How do stars and constellations work?

Complete focus sessions to earn stars. Organize your sessions by project to build constellations that represent your progress.

How can I back up my progress?

Use the backup export option in the app to save a copy of your data. Keep your exported backup somewhere safe.

How can I report a bug?

Email offlinelab000@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description. Screenshots are helpful, but optional.

Privacy & your data

Starlit is designed to work without account registration. Focus history and project data are stored locally on your device. The app does not require advertising or analytics tracking to function. If you contact support by email, the information you choose to send will be used to respond to your request.

For privacy questions, email offlinelab000@gmail.com.
