Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Starlit is a focus timer designed to help you build productive habits through focus sessions, stars, and constellations. We respect your privacy and aim to keep your personal information under your control.

1. Information stored on your device

Starlit stores your focus sessions, goals, notes, preferences, and progress locally on your device. No account is required to use the app.

2. Data collection and tracking

Starlit does not require registration and does not use advertising or third-party analytics for tracking. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Internet access and purchases

Core focus timer features work offline. Purchases and downloads are processed by Apple's App Store under Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices. We do not directly receive your payment card details.

4. Backups and data deletion

You may export a backup using the app's backup feature. You are responsible for where you choose to store or share exported files. Removing the app may remove locally stored app data, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.

5. Support communications

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and information you choose to include in your message. We use that information only to respond to your support request.

6. Children's privacy

Starlit is not designed to solicit personal information from children. If you have a privacy concern, please contact us.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its privacy practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or support, email offlinelab000@gmail.com.
